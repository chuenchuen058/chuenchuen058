## 👋 About me

I build things that move — and the software and electronics that make them work.

- 🎓 **2nd-year Biomedical Engineering** student at **King's College London**
- 🏭 Former **Manufacturing & Design Engineering intern** at **Plexus**, Penang
- 🤖 Into **robotics, embedded systems and hardware bring-up** (and building watches ⌚)
- 🔭 Currently working on **UnibotsMarkII** and **Denisse**, my personal AI assistant
- 🎯 Looking for **robotics & automation** internships in **Penang** and beyond
- 💬 Ask me about **ESP32s, motor drivers, state machines, or Fusion 360**
